the problem

Prices can drop while you sleep. xlmflow repays for you, from a reserve only you control

how the repay works

Started by the price, paid from your reserve

  1. 01 / repay rule

    Repays just enough

    At the trigger, the vault repays debt back to a 150% ratio. Never more than your reserve or your limit. Try a price on the right

    min(gap, reserve, limit)
  2. 02 / watch

    Lasna watches the price

    A contract on Reactive Lasna reads every price update from Base Sepolia. At $1,350 or lower, it asks for a repay

    onlySystem · requestCallbackV_1_0
  3. 03 / callback

    Only trusted calls get in

    The repay arrives through Reactive's callback proxy. The vault accepts it only when it comes from your Lasna contract

    proxy check · sender check
  4. 04 / guards

    Late or repeated calls are skipped

    Only the newest price counts, only once, and only while it is under 15 minutes old. Anything else is logged and skipped

    newest · once · 15 min
  5. 05 / reserve

    Your reserve stays yours

    Each borrower has a separate reserve, and only you can withdraw it. Close the loan to get your collateral and leftover reserve back

    fund · withdraw · close

how it works

Four simple steps

  1. 01

    Open

    Lock collateral and borrow mUSD, at 160% or more

  2. 02

    Protect

    Fund a reserve, set a limit and turn protection on

  3. 03

    React

    The price hits $1,350 and Lasna asks for a repay

  4. 04

    Repay

    The vault repays from your reserve. All 3 transactions are linked
